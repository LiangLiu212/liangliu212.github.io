I am a researcher working on neutrino-nucleus interactions and nuclear
structure methods for neutrino oscillation experiments.

My current work focuses on building a self-consistent framework for
neutrino-nucleus scattering by extending the Liège Intranuclear Cascade
(INCL++) to include leptonic projectile channels. This unifies the primary
interaction vertex and final-state interactions within a single nuclear
model, eliminating the mismatch between separate ground-state and cascade
treatments in conventional generators.

On the theory side, I am interested in comparing impulse-approximation
methods (spectral function, local Fermi gas) against ab-initio many-body
calculations with chiral currents, with the goal of assessing how well
factorized approaches describe binding energy and Fermi motion in QE
neutrino scattering.

**Software:** [NeutrinoGEN](https://github.com/LiangLiu212) — a modular
weighted-event generator with PDG-backed particle data, pluggable form
factors (dipole and z-expansion), and a Dirac algebra layer for
channel-agnostic current construction.
