## Research Experience

**Measurement of Charged-Current Pionless Interactions at MicroBooNE** *(2024 – present)*  
Cross-section measurements of the dominant neutrino interaction channel for the SBN program, with direct relevance to DUNE oscillation analyses.

**Interfacing GENIE with the INCL Model** *(2024 – present)*  
Integrating the Liège Intranuclear Cascade into GENIE for a self-consistent treatment of primary interactions and final-state interactions within a single nuclear framework.

**CP Violation and ΔI = ½ Rule in Hyperon Decays at BESIII** *(Nov 2021 – Dec 2023)*  
Precision measurement of decay asymmetry parameters in Ξ⁻ → Λπ⁻ and Λ → pπ⁻/nπ⁰ using 10 billion J/ψ events. Constructed four CP asymmetry observables; applied a boosted decision tree for photon discrimination; performed a maximum-likelihood fit using helicity-frame joint angular amplitudes. Developed a GPU-based CUDA package achieving a 20× speedup. Resulted in doctoral thesis and PRL publication (lead analyst).

**First Measurement of J/ψ → Σ⁻Σ̄⁺ at BESIII** *(Sep 2018 – Dec 2023)*  
Measured branching fraction and production parameters to test flavor-SU(3) symmetry and pQCD predictions. Developed a data-driven method for anti-neutron EMC response simulation, subsequently adopted by five BESIII analyses.

**Anti-Neutron Nuclear Interactions at BESIII** *(Sep 2018 – Dec 2023)*  
Using J/ψ → pn̄π⁻, identified and reconstructed exclusive reactions n̄ + A → p̄ + p + X and n̄ + A → p̄ + 2p + X′ produced by anti-neutrons interacting with the beryllium, gold, carbon, and oxygen of the beam pipe and MDC. Developed a custom Geant4 physics process to simulate anti-neutron–nucleus interactions.

**Data-Driven Anti-Neutron Detector Simulation at BESIII** *(Sep 2018 – 2021)*  
Developed a data-driven method to simulate anti-neutron detector response in the BESIII EMC, replacing the inadequate Geant4 hadronic models. Both detection efficiency and shower observables are sampled directly from data. Adopted by three published BESIII analyses.
