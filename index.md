## Selected Research

**Measurement of charged-current pionless interactions at MicroBooNE** *(2024 – present)*  
Cross-section measurements critical for the SBN program and DUNE oscillation analyses.

**GENIE × INCL integration** *(2024 – present)*  
Interfacing the GENIE neutrino generator with the Liège Intranuclear Cascade model for a self-consistent treatment of final-state interactions.

**CP violation and Δ*I* = ½ rule in hyperon decays at BESIII** *(2021 – 2023)*  
Precision measurement of decay asymmetry parameters in Ξ⁻ → Λπ⁻ and Λ → pπ⁻/nπ⁰. Developed a GPU-based CUDA package for maximum-likelihood fits, achieving a 20× speedup. Resulted in doctoral thesis and BESIII thesis award.

**First measurement of *J*/ψ → Σ⁻Σ̄⁺ at BESIII** *(2018 – 2023)*  
Measurement of branching fraction and production parameters to test flavor-SU(3) symmetry and pQCD predictions. Developed a data-driven method for anti-neutron detector response simulation, adopted by five BESIII analyses.

**Anti-neutron nuclear interactions at BESIII** *(2018 – 2023)*  
Identified and reconstructed exclusive reactions n̄ + A → p̄ + p + X and n̄ + A → p̄ + 2p + X′ using 10 billion J/ψ events. Extracted nucleon-nucleon correlations inside nuclei and developed a custom Geant4 physics process.
