## Research

My work focuses on neutrino-nucleus interactions, nuclear structure, and
event generation for neutrino oscillation experiments.

Current projects:

- **NeutrinoGEN** — a modular weighted-event neutrino interaction generator
  with PDG-backed particle data, pluggable form factors (dipole, z-expansion),
  and a Dirac/Clifford algebra layer for channel-agnostic current construction
- **INCL++ extension** — adding leptonic projectile channels to the Liège
  Intranuclear Cascade for a self-consistent treatment of primary interaction
  and final-state interactions in a unified nuclear framework
- **Ab-initio nuclear response** — comparing impulse-approximation and
  ab-initio many-body calculations with chiral currents for CCQE on ¹²C
